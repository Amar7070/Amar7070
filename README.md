## Featured Projects

### 🍔 [BigBite: AI-Powered Real-Time Food Delivery Platform](https://github.com/Amar7070/BigBite_food)
A role-based delivery platform with separate customer, restaurant, and rider workflows.
- Autonomous **LangChain + LangGraph** agent powered by **Google Gemini** that manages carts, places orders, navigates the app, and submits reviews from natural-language and voice commands
- Real-time order and rider tracking with **Socket.IO**, **React Leaflet**, and Haversine-based geolocation (25 km rider discovery, live GPS broadcasting)
- JWT authentication, RESTful APIs, and **Razorpay** payments

`React` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `Socket.IO` `LangChain` `LangGraph` `Gemini API` `Razorpay`

### ⏱️ [Time & Productivity Analysis System](https://productivity-tracker-aky.vercel.app/)
A full-stack productivity tracker with analytics-driven insights.
- Hierarchical project → task → subtask management with **JWT auth, Google OAuth, and role-based access control**
- Dashboard with dynamic Focus Scores, activity heatmaps, productivity trends, and goal tracking (Recharts)
- Containerized with **Docker** and automated build/deploy via **Jenkins CI/CD**; integrates Cloudinary, Firebase, and transactional email

`React` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `Docker` `Jenkins`
